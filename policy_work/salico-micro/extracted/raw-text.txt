PART I — GENERAL CONTRACT PROVISIONS 	STANDARD PROVISIONS
1. Consideration & Entire Contract: This Policy, together with the Schedule of Benefits and approved Endorsements, constitutes the entire
contract between the Company and the Insured. All indemnities are payable strictly in consideration of the advance payment of stipulated
premium and subject to the terms, limitations, and exceptions herein contained.
2. Policy Inception & Effective Date: Insurance coverage takes effect at 12:01 A.M. standard time at the residence of the Insured on the
Effective Date stated in the Schedule of Benefits. The policy remains in force until the stipulated Renewal Date, subject to the Grace Period.
3. Grace Period: A grace period of thirty-one (31) calendar days is granted for the payment of each renewal premium falling due after the initial
premium. During this period, the policy continues in full force, provided that any unpaid premium due is deducted from any benefit payable
during the grace period.
4. Notice of Claim: Written notice of claim must be furnished to the Company within ten (10) calendar days from the commencement of
hospital confinement or occurrence of loss, or as soon thereafter as is reasonably possible.
PART II — DEFINITIONS 	CONTRACT DEFINITIONS
1. Insured: The eligible person named in the Schedule who has attained at least eighteen (18) years of age and not more than fifty-nine (59) years
of age on the Effective Date of this Policy. EXECUTABLE
2. In-Patient: A person who is formally admitted to a licensed Hospital as a registered bed patient and continuously confined for a minimum
duration of at least twenty-four (24) consecutive hours. EXECUTABLE
3. Sickness: Any disease or pathological illness first manifested, contracted, and commencing after the active Coverage Commencement Date of
this policy. EXECUTABLE
4. Injury: Accidental bodily trauma occurring while this policy is in force, effected solely and directly by external, violent, and accidental means
evidenced by visible contusion or wound, independent of all other causes. TEXT REFERENCE
5. Pre-Existing Condition: Any physical condition, disorder, or symptom for which medical advice, diagnosis, care, or treatment was received, or
for which a physician was consulted at any time prior to the Effective Date or Reinstatement Date. EXECUTABLE
6. Hospital: An institution legally licensed as a hospital which operates primarily for the inpatient reception, care, and medical treatment of ailing
or injured persons; maintains 24-hour graduate nursing services; has licensed physicians available at all times; possesses organized diagnostic and
major surgical operating facilities; and is not primarily a clinic, rest home, sanatorium, or substance rehabilitation center. TEXT REFERENCE
7. Medical Necessity: Hospital confinement, care, or surgical procedure certified by the attending Physician confirming that safe and efficacious
medical treatment could not have been provided on an outpatient basis. TEXT REFERENCE
8. Policy Year & Currency: A period of three hundred sixty-five (365) consecutive days commencing on the Effective Date. All financial benefits,
sub-limits, and deductibles are expressed in Nepalese Rupees (NPR). EXECUTABLE

PART III — ELIGIBILITY & TERMINATION 	COVERAGE TERMS
1. Entry Age Limits: Coverage may only be effected for persons between the ages of eighteen (18) and fifty-nine (59) completed years on the
Effective Date.
2. Age Expiration: Inpatient surgical expense and sickness hospitalization benefits terminate automatically when the Insured attains sixty-five
(65) years of age. Accidental death, dismemberment, and medical expense reimbursements terminate upon attaining seventy (70) years of age.
PART IV — INPATIENT HOSPITALIZATION BENEFITS 	HOSPITALIZATION
1. Inpatient Indemnity: When an Insured is confined within a licensed Hospital as a registered In-Patient for medical necessity under
continuous physician care, the Company reimburses room, board, and hospital nursing expenses subject to the applicable sub-limits.
2. Room & Board Daily Limit: Eligible room and board charges are covered up to a daily limit equal to the lower of: (a) NPR 5,000 per day;
or (b) 1.0% of the Hospitalization Sum Insured per day.
3. Intensive Care Unit (ICU) Sub-Limit: Medically necessary confinement in an Intensive Care Unit is covered up to a daily limit of NPR
10,000 per day.
4. Maximum Confinement Period: Daily hospital income or confinement indemnity is payable for a maximum aggregate duration of fifty-two
(52) weeks (364 calendar days) per policy year.
PART V — IN-HOSPITAL SURGICAL EXPENSE BENEFITS 	SURGICAL COVERAGE
1. Surgical Procedure Indemnity: When an Insured undergoes a surgical operation performed by a licensed surgeon during covered hospital
confinement, the Company reimburses the surgeon's fee, anesthetist's fee, and operating room rent, up to the Percentage of Maximum Surgical
Benefit set forth in the Surgical Schedule below.

2. Surgical Session Rule: If multiple surgical procedures are performed during the same operative session, the indemnity payable shall be
restricted solely to the highest percentage applicable among the procedures performed.
3. Annual Procedure Frequency: Benefit in respect of any covered surgical procedure is payable only once during any single Policy Year.
4. Annual Surgical Aggregate Ceiling: Total surgical indemnities paid within any single Policy Year shall not exceed two hundred percent
(200% / double) of the Maximum Surgical Benefit (NPR 400,000).

PART VI — WAITING PERIODS & ELIMINATION 	TEMPORAL BOUNDS
1. Initial Sickness Waiting Period: Coverage for Sickness becomes effective on the thirty-first (31st) day following the Policy Effective Date
or Reinstatement Date. No benefits are payable for any illness contracted or manifested during the initial 30 days. Accidental injuries carry zero
(0) waiting days.
2. Specific Named Ailments Waiting Period: Hospital confinement or surgical treatment for tonsils, adenoids, hernia, or intervertebral disc
disease is excluded until the Insured has been continuously covered under this policy for at least one hundred twenty (120) days immediately
preceding confinement.
3. Confinement Elimination Period: The first one (1) day (24 hours) of hospital confinement due to Sickness constitutes an Elimination Period
for which daily benefit is not payable.
PART VII — GENERAL EXCEPTIONS & CARVE-OUTS 	EXCLUSIONS
1. Pre-Existing Conditions: Pre-existing medical conditions, diagnosed or treated prior to the Effective Date, are strictly excluded from
coverage.
2. Cosmetic & Plastic Surgery: Cosmetic or plastic surgery is excluded, except when necessitated solely by an Accidental Bodily Injury
occurring while this policy is in force.
3. Dental Care & Surgery: Dental care, fillings, or oral surgery are excluded, except when treatment is required for damage to sound natural
teeth caused directly by an Accidental Bodily Injury.
4. Maternity & Obstetrical Care: Hospital confinement or surgical treatment resulting directly or indirectly from pregnancy, childbirth,
miscarriage, abortion, or related complications is excluded. The obstetrical schedule percentage is explicitly None (0%).
5. Hazardous Sports & Alternative Medicine: Excluded are injuries sustained during competitive racing, mountaineering, potholing,
paragliding, bungee jumping, parachuting, scuba diving; stays in sanatoriums; and treatments in Ayurvedic, herbal, or acupuncture facilities.
PART VIII — ACCIDENT MEDICAL EXPENSE & COORDINATION 	MEDICAL REIMBURSEMENT
1. Medical Expense Deductible: Accident medical expense reimbursement is subject to a deductible of NPR 500 per claim. The Company
reimburses eligible expenses in excess of NPR 500 up to NPR 50,000.
2. Non-Duplication: If the Insured is entitled to reimbursement under any statutory scheme or other insurance, the Company is liable only for
the excess non-reimbursed reasonable charges.

3. STRUCTURAL SIMPLIFICATIONS & RULE ATOMICITY 	SIMPLIFICATIONS
• Cosmetic & Dental Carve-Outs: The source policy excludes cosmetic and dental care in general, but carves out an exception when required solely for

conditions (e.g., Is_Cosmetic and not Is_Accident -> Reject).
• Named Ailment Waiting Periods: Sickness carries a general 31-day waiting period, while Form 77-D specifies a 120-day waiting period for hernia,
tonsils, adenoids, and disc disease. In the rule engine, this is handled via an explicit ICD-10 diagnosis group match rule (R09).
